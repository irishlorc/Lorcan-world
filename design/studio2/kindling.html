How to ask for a deposit without it feeling awkward.

A deposit isn't a sign you don't trust someone. It's how you book the time, order the materials and turn down other work for them. Said like that, most clients agree before you've finished the sentence.

The awkward part is usually timing. Ask on the quote, not after the yes. It reads as how you work, not a condition you thought of later.

Copy this line

"To lock in your dates, I take 30% when you accept the quote. The rest is due when the job's done."

Three things to do before Friday

1Send the invoices for anything finished this week, while it's fresh for you and them.

2Photograph every receipt in your van door pocket. Yes, that one too.

3Reply to the quote you've been avoiding. A short no is kinder than a long silence.
