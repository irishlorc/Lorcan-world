How it works

From first coffee to launch.

Getting a great website shouldn't be complicated. I take care of the creative and technical heavy lifting, keeping you involved where it matters.

  1. 01

    Coffee & Introductions

    We meet virtually for a relaxed coffee, discuss your business, your ideas and what you'd like to achieve.

  2. 02

    Content & Planning

    I gather what we need, organise the content and map out the structure of your website.

  3. 03

    First Draft

    I get to work on the design and build, turning our ideas into a working website you can explore.

  4. 04

    Review Together

    We meet virtually, walk through the website and discuss any changes or refinements.

  5. 05

    Domain & Setup

    I handle the technical setup, including connecting your domain and getting everything ready to go live.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Your new website goes live, ready for the world to see.
