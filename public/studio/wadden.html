The Studio

01 · Maritime robotics · Independent work

Eyes on the water line.

A fictional Dutch engineering company whose autonomous robots inspect dikes, flood defences and the water infrastructure that keeps a low country dry.

WADDEN is built to be trusted by people who inspect dikes for a living. The identity borrows from engineering drawings and safety signage: a navy field, a single warning yellow, mono type for anything measured. Photography stays documentary, shot in the flat light of the Wadden coast, so the robot reads as working equipment rather than a gadget.

Industry
Maritime robotics
Audience
Water boards, infrastructure operators, engineering firms and municipalities.
Status
Self-initiated, fictional brand
Disciplines
Identity · Campaign · Print · Social · Web
Palette

Photography

Documentary, never staged. Flat North Sea light, wet basalt and real weather, because the people buying this inspect dikes for a living and can spot a set-up shot instantly.

Identity

The W reads as two folds of water running down a dike. Yellow means caution on a dike, so it is rationed: the flag in the logo, the bumpers on the machine and very little else.

Print

Brochure and stationery share one grid and one voice: plain engineering language, big photography, specification in mono. Documents that will be photocopied, filed and read on site.

Web

The site behaves like a control room: a live inspection feed, the run explained in three steps and a cross-section of the dike that shows what each sensor finds. It sells the outcome, an engineer who knows the state of every metre.
