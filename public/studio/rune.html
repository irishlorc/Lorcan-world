The Studio

03 · Premium audio · Independent work

Sound, without compromise.

A fictional premium audio company making wireless headphones and home speakers that feel considered, tactile and effortless, and desirable before they are switched on.

RUNE is about restraint. Charcoal, off-white and real metal carry the brand, and violet is allowed once per layout, the way the light ring sits on the headphones. Every image is lit with one hard source on stone, plaster or fabric, so materials do the persuading and the typography stays quiet.

Industry
Premium audio
Audience
Music lovers, designers and people who care how their things are made.
Status
Self-initiated, fictional brand
Disciplines
Identity · Product imagery · Packaging · Outdoor · Web
Palette

Campaign

The object first, then the craft: the headphones on stone in one hard light, then the machined yoke up close. The type stays quiet so the product is the loudest thing in the frame.

Identity

Charcoal, off-white and real metal. Violet appears once per layout, the way the light ring sits on the headphones, and nowhere else.

Editorial

The brand earns its place in a magazine by being about listening, not specifications. A concrete room, a slice of winter sun and one record played start to finish.
