The Studio

02 · Small-business software · Independent work

Less administration. More room to run your business.

Fictional software that takes invoices, payment reminders, follow-ups and everyday admin off the people running small businesses, without adding another thing to learn.

Kindling had to feel like a helpful colleague, not another dashboard. The brand pairs a confident sans with an italic serif for the human aside, and speaks in the words a tradesperson would actually send. Photography shows the end of a working day, warm and unposed, so the product is always about the person it gives time back to.

Industry
Small-business software
Audience
Freelancers, tradespeople, café owners and independent professionals.
Status
Self-initiated, fictional brand
Disciplines
Identity · Editorial · Advertising · Product UI · Web
Palette

Voice

Small-business owners don't want software, they want their evenings back. So the brand shows real people at the end of a working day and the actual words a reminder would say, never a floating dashboard.

Identity

A bold sans for the plain facts and an italic serif for the human aside. Purple and cream are the paper; green only ever means something got done.

Editorial

The newsletter reads like a small magazine rather than a product update: one lead story, a line to copy, three things to do before Friday. Its readers owe it nothing, so it has to earn the open.

Web

A masthead, a big headline and a collage of real moments. The website is set like the newsletter, so the brand feels the same wherever you meet it.
