The Possibilities Studio

Don't imagine what I could make. Have a look.

Four fictional companies, each given its own identity and a body of finished work: an engineering firm, a software business, an audio brand and a furniture maker.

These aren't projects I was hired to make. They're examples of what I could bring to the table.

01

WADDEN

A fictional Dutch engineering company whose autonomous robots inspect dikes, flood defences and water infrastructure.

Maritime robotics

The thinkingYellow means caution on a dike, so it's rationed: the flag in the logo, the bumpers on the machine and very little else. The photography stays documentary, with flat North Sea light, wet basalt and real weather, because the people buying this inspect dikes for a living and can spot a staged shot.

02

Kindling

Fictional software that takes invoices, follow-ups and everyday admin off small-business owners.

Small-business software

The thinkingSmall-business owners don't want software, they want their evenings back. So the brand talks like a helpful colleague, sets its headlines like a magazine and shows the actual words a tradesperson would send, never a floating dashboard.

03

RUNE

A fictional premium audio company making headphones and home speakers that feel considered, tactile and effortless.

Premium audio

The product, packaging and campaign work is being reshot as photography and will join the identity here soon.

The thinkingBold, minimal and architectural: charcoal and off-white, real metal, and violet used once per layout, like the light ring on the headphones.

04

FOLD

A fictional Dutch furniture company making modular pieces for modern homes.

Modular furniture

The collection, catalogue, campaign and showroom work is being shot now and will join the identity here soon.

The thinkingThe logo is built the way the furniture is: four letters made of folded, interlocking blocks. Terracotta leads and forest green supports, with cream and charcoal holding it together like plaster and steel in a canal house.

Like what you see? Imagine it with your brand.
