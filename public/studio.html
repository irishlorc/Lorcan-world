The Possibilities Studio

Don't imagine what I could make. Have a look.

A collection of self-initiated creative concepts. No client briefs, no committees, no twelve rounds of feedback. Just ideas brought to life.

These aren't projects I was hired to make. They're examples of what I could bring to the table.

Two fictional companies. WADDEN is a Dutch robotics start-up whose robots inspect the dikes that keep the country dry. It runs through three collections: the identity, the launch and the pitch, the way a real brand would. HEARTH is a calm AI assistant for small businesses, shown in its newsletter, website and product.

01

Brand Worlds

A complete identity for a fictional Dutch robotics company that builds dike-inspection robots.

Concept · WADDEN

The thinkingThe W is two folds of water running down a dike, and the orange stroke that breaks away is the alert. That orange is only ever used for alerts, and everything else, down to the field notebook, comes from the place the robots work.

02

Editorial & Newsletters

Kindling, the weekly newsletter for a fictional AI assistant that looks after small-business admin.

Concept · HEARTH

The thinkingOne lead story, two short items and one number. It reads like a small magazine rather than a product update, because its readers are busy people who owe it nothing.

03

Campaigns & Social

The launch of Kwelder, Wadden's first robot, across social, recruitment and outdoor.

Concept · WADDEN

The thinkingEvery piece makes one point and hands over to the next: what it is, why it matters, who it's for. The billboard is the one you'd remember on the drive home.

04

Presentations & Pitch Decks

A pilot proposal for Dutch water boards, the people who'd actually buy Kwelder.

Concept · WADDEN

The thinkingA deck for an engineer's room: one idea per slide, a timeline instead of a wall of bullets, and a first step small enough to say yes to.

05

Digital Experiences

Hearth's website, mobile view and the product's morning dashboard.

Concept · HEARTH

The thinkingThe promise on the homepage is the first screen of the product: the morning's to-do, already done, waiting for a nod.
