About

A little about me.

I'm Lorcán. Most people call me Lor.

I'm originally from Dublin, spent nearly nine years living and working in the Netherlands, and have never been particularly good at sticking to one thing.

I've written eight books, built apps, made music videos, worked with some brilliant brands, and found myself doing everything from creative direction to commercial development.

These days, I'm involved in the AI world, working on the commercial side of Arkintel, a Dutch technology company. I also build my own little collection of digital products and pursue whatever interesting idea decides to occupy my brain next.

I've been exploring AI for years, long before everyone started putting it in their LinkedIn headline. But technology is only one part of what interests me.

I enjoy the business of making things happen just as much as making the things themselves. Whether that's developing an idea, bringing people together, designing something, or figuring out how to turn a conversation into an opportunity.

Outside of all that, I enjoy meeting interesting people, exploring new places, and having a good laugh along the way.

I suppose the common thread is curiosity. I've just never seen much reason to limit where it takes me.

If something here caught your attention, feel free to say hello.
